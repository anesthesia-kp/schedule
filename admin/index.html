🎲 Simulator
Tools for filling a month quickly so you can see how the site behaves with data in it. They are here, and not on the Schedule Grid, because they are for testing — and because the real thing is not this. A rules-driven engine that reads approved requests is what will eventually build a schedule (§44); auto-populate is a crude ancestor of it and must not grow into it.
Rehearsal mode
One switch for every tool below. This is not a sandbox. Everything here writes the real schedule, and everyone sees the result immediately — the switch exists so that filling or wiping a month is always two deliberate acts instead of one stray click. It is in memory, so it turns itself off when you reload.
Fill
Gives each shift to whoever has done it least over the rolling 12 months (real schedule + the baseline on the Schedule Grid). Existing assignments are kept. One shift per person per day — the simulator never doubles anyone up (§45), so what it produces is easy to read.
Wipe
Clear Month removes every assignment for the month you are viewing, for everybody. There is no undo, and staff see it at once. It is parked here for now because it is a testing tool; when the site is in real use it needs a better answer than a button.
📈 Reports ADMIN ONLY
Per doctor, for any period you choose: how many of each shift they did with overnight call first, every overnight call listed by date, and a comparison with the group adjusted for FTE. Opens in its own tab using the Vacation Auction's report stylesheet, so both sites produce identical-looking paperwork.
Which shifts count as overnight call?
You set this — nothing is worked out automatically. It cannot be: shifts that cross midnight would sweep in RCH-ICU B, a night ICU shift, and everything in the call family would sweep in Eye Call (07:30–15:30) and Call 12 AM (07:30–19:30), which are daytime. Anything left unticked is simply not overnight call, whatever its name or hours suggest.
Who counts toward the group comparison?
Everyone counts unless you untick them. Untick per diem and locums — they are still scheduled, still reported on in full, and still get their own numbers; they just don't affect anyone else's group average. Anyone with no FTE set is left out of the average automatically, because there is no way to work out their share, and the report says so on their page.

This is the one place the call baseline is included.
