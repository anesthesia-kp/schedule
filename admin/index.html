⚠️ This month is stored in the old format — it is read-only until you convert it
Assignments used to live in one document per month. They now live in one document per day, so that a month can hold the whole group without hitting Firestore's size limit, and so two people editing different weeks stop fighting over the same record.

Nothing is deleted by converting. It copies the month across in a single step — either all of it lands or none of it does — and the old record is kept exactly as it is, as a backup.

Everything below is showing you the real, current assignments. You just can't change them until this month has moved across.
Call fairness: fairness is measured on a rolling 12-month average — the last 12 months of the actual saved schedule, plus each person's starting baseline below. This feeds the fairness view on the Reports page and the engine's choice of who has done a shift least. The baseline stays here because it is real fairness data.

Quotas
A quota is a named set of shifts that share one combined total for a day — D and D10 flexing against each other because their sum is fixed. A shift may sit in more than one quota. The total uses the same rule list as staffing, so it can differ on weekends and holidays. Auto-populate fills to it: each shift's own minimum first, then each quota topped up across its member shifts to its total — and a quota the minimums already exceed is reported as a conflict, never trimmed.
▸Add a quota
Name it for what it covers. It keeps its internal id if you rename it later.

Rules
A rule is a standing expectation — nobody starts a shift within ten hours of finishing one — checked against every month. You build as many as you like, each pointed at everyone, a category, a group, named people, or any combination. A rule never blocks anything. It warns, it is listed in the report below, and a person decides.
Nothing in this app is ever hard-blocked. Severity decides where a conflict sits in the report and how loud the confirmation is — Serious asks for a deliberate yes, Warn is named in the ordinary confirmation, Note is listed and never raised. None of the three refuses a change.
▸Add a rule
It is born covering everyone at Warn. Open it to narrow who it covers and how loud it is.

Calendar Feeds
A private link per doctor that their phone's calendar subscribes to once, after which their own shifts and approved vacation appear alongside everything else in their life, and keep up to date on their own. Nobody sees anyone else's schedule.
⛔ NOT FOR DOCTORS YET — two things must happen first, and neither is on this page.
1 · The address below does not answer yet. A calendar subscription needs something live to answer it, and GitHub Pages only serves files. The small hosted endpoint that returns these feeds is not deployed.
2 · The link list must be locked down first. Until dailysched/feedTokens is admin-only in the security rules, any signed-in user can read it — and a readable link list means anyone can read anyone's calendar. Those rules live in the auction repo and are closed without your specific say-so.
And the release gate itself is still shut — your ruling: build it, but no doctor is told until the schedule is complete.
▸Who has a link
A link is a long random string that stands in for signing in, because calendar apps cannot sign in. Replacing someone's link breaks whatever they already subscribed to — which is how you revoke one that has leaked, and why it asks first.
Feeds do not refresh themselves yet — that is the next build.
Print
A clean sheet for the wall or the pocket: one sheet per site for a single day, or a whole week at a time. Choose which shifts belong on it once — the choice is saved and used every time after that.
A week runs Sunday to Saturday, like every other week on this site. Pick any day in a week and the whole week prints.
▸Which shifts
Grouped by family. A shift added to the catalog later is on the sheet unless you take it off here — so a new shift is never quietly missing from the paper.
Groups
A group is the unit you name — OB, Pediatric, ICU. Each one has two subgroups: its MD side and its CRNA side. You add a person to the group; being an MD or a CRNA decides which side they land on.
This page decides who can be assigned to what. A shift lists the groups — or one side of a group, or named individuals — that can work it, on the Shift Eligibility page. Everyone should be in at least one group; anybody in none can only be given work by being named on a shift individually.
Shift Eligibility
Who can work each shift — by group, by one side of a group, or by naming a person. Auto-populate, cell assignment and request approval all respect this.

Shift Catalog
Every shift type in the system, and when each one runs. Times are entered here and nowhere else — they are what the rules, the compatibility check and eventually the scheduling engine all read (§44). A shift with no time is not a tidy gap; it is an input nothing downstream can reason about.
▸Add a shift
Runs to
24-hour clock. Type 0730, 7:30 or 07.30 — all the same thing. Leave both blank if you do not know the times yet; blank is a real answer and nothing will guess for you (§38).

▸Paste a list of shifts
One shift per line. Separate the columns with a tab or a comma: label · start · end · site — everything after the label is optional. A header row is ignored if you paste one. Times use the same 24-hour rules as everywhere else: 0730, 7:30 or 07.30.
Nothing is written until you have read the preview and pressed the button under it (§3).
▸Holidays
The eleven US federal holidays are worked out automatically; add or remove any you like. A staffing rule can point at “holidays”, so this list is what that rule reads.
Both the real date and the federal “observed” day count (§49a) — when a holiday falls at the weekend, the observed day shifts to the Friday or Monday, and both days are treated as holidays: the hospital runs on the real day, and the observed day is the one most of the group is off. Remove either one if that is not how your group works.

⚙️ Engine
How shifts get assigned. Preview shows exactly what the engine would do for the month on the Schedule Grid — nothing is written until you press Apply. It follows every rule that is on: staffing minimums, quota totals, the post-call day, one site per day, and every rule you have made. What it cannot place, it tells you, with the rule that stopped it. You can always place a shift yourself below — a rule warns, it never blocks.
Draft or published
Run the engine
Each shift is filled to its staffing minimum, then each quota to its total, giving every shift to whoever has done it least over the rolling twelve months — after the people who asked for it (approved requests, strongest first) and the people who said they are available. An approved avoid is kept. One shift per person per day. Apply adds what it planned to whatever each cell holds at the moment it writes — an assignment made since you pressed Preview is kept, and the engine removes nothing. A person marked off since you previewed is skipped for that day: the day off wins, the shift is not placed, and the skip is counted and named in the result rather than passed over. Preview waits until this month has finished loading — until it is here the button is off and says so, because a month that has not arrived yet looks empty, and the engine would treat everybody as free every day. And if a day would end up with more people on a shift than that shift asks for, the plan and the Apply question both say so, day by day. A preview goes out of date the moment anything it was worked out from changes — a rule, a quota, a shift, who may work what, a group, a request, vacation, the month itself, or somebody editing a day in this month. When that happens the plan is marked here with what changed, Apply is switched off, and one press of Preview again works it out afresh.

⚙️ Settings — what counts as overnight call · who's in the comparison
Which shifts count as overnight call?
You set this — nothing is worked out automatically. It cannot be: shifts that cross midnight would sweep in RCH-ICU B, a night ICU shift, and everything in the call family would sweep in Eye Call (07:30–15:30) and Call 12 AM (07:30–19:30), which are daytime. Anything left unticked is simply not overnight call, whatever its name or hours suggest.
Who counts toward the group comparison?
The group decides this now. Mark a group Not in averages on the Groups page — that is where per diem and locums belong — and everyone in it is left out of the group average. They are still scheduled, still reported on in full, and still get their own numbers.
Ticking somebody here against what their groups say makes them an override: it is kept, it is labelled on this page, on their group's card and inside every report, and it stays until you change it back. Anyone with no FTE set is left out automatically whatever is ticked, because there is no way to work out their share.
