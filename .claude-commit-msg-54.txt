Build 54 (admin) — Stage 1: shift TIMES and SITES, entered in the Shift Catalog

DECISIONS §17, §18, §20, §38. Admin only; staff untouched at 27. No rules change.

WHY

  Owner, 16 Aug: "I have no way to enter shift times. This should probably be built into
  the shift catalog. I will need a way to edit times for every shift in there now and also
  to add times when I'm adding a new shift." Asked whether location was part of it: yes.

  It is the bottleneck, and §44 sharpened it. A shift with no time is not a tidy gap in a
  record — it is an input that the rules, the compatibility check and eventually the
  scheduling engine cannot reason about at all.

TIMES

  · A 24-hour field, on every shift in the list and on the Add form.
  · NOT <input type="time">. That control renders AM/PM on a US-locale browser and cannot
    be forced out of it (§17), so these are plain text inputs with their own parser. The
    page carries a comment saying so, and the suite asserts the comment survives — this is
    exactly the kind of thing a later reader "helpfully" swaps back.
  · Accepts 0730 · 7:30 · 07.30 · 730 — all the same thing. Refuses 25:00, 7:5, 12:75 and
    anything else, tells the admin what a valid time looks like, and KEEPS THE PREVIOUS
    VALUE. Nothing is half-written and nothing is silently coerced into something nobody
    typed.
  · Blank is a real answer, not zero and not a gap to fill in helpfully (§38).
  · Duration is computed and HONOURS MIDNIGHT. Call 16 at 15:30-07:30 reads 16h; Call 24 at
    07:30-07:30 reads 24h, not 0h — a non-positive difference always means the next day.
    Midnight-crossers are marked.
  · HALF A TIME IS CALLED OUT. A start with no end looks set and reasons like blank, which
    is worse than blank. Flagged in the row, counted separately in the summary, and
    confirmed against when adding.
  · A per-shift "not sure yet" flag keeps provisional times visibly amber (§17). This is
    what the four SUSPECT call times need — C2PA, C2AP, Call 8, PCV Call.

  §17 is respected on the other axis too: LABELS AND TIMES ARE SEPARATE DATA. A shift called
  "4 to 8" keeps that label and stores 15:30-19:30 underneath. Neither is derived from the
  other — the label is what the group calls it, the times are what the machine reads.

SITES — §18

  Oakland or Richmond on every shift, on both the row and the Add form. §18 makes site a
  property of a person's WHOLE DAY — a mismatch between two shifts is impossible regardless
  of their hours, because it is not a timing question — so it is near-mandatory data.

  The list falls back to the owner's two in code and is stored in catalog.sites once an
  admin sets one. Nothing is written on page load. That is deliberately the same shape as
  the overnight-call tag: a default that is HIS ruling rather than a guess, no one-shot
  self-marking migration (defect 24), and no hardcoded option list in the markup
  (defect 21).

A PROGRESS BAR THAT MEANS SOMETHING

  The catalog now says how many shifts have times, how many have a site, and how many are
  half-set. Per §44 that is the real measure of how close the project is to an engine that
  can run: untimed shifts are unusable input.

THE CATALOG NOW LOCKS — §20

  §20 lists the Shift Catalog among the pages that get a master switch and default LOCKED;
  it never got one. It does now. The refusal sits at the top of every mutating handler —
  times, site, capacity, family, role, add, delete — so calling setShiftTime() straight
  from the console while locked writes nothing and says so. A lock that only dims buttons
  is not a lock.

WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT HERE

  §17 says "no admin shift may end after 17:30, which is a validation rule the app should
  enforce". It is not enforced yet, and that is on purpose: knowing which shifts are "admin"
  means either deriving it from the label — the exact mistake §27 exists to prevent — or
  pointing at a tag the admin has set. It belongs in the rules section (stage 5) as a rule
  instance, not as a guess baked in here.
