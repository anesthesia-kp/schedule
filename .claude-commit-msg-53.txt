Build 53 (admin) — Testing section: auto-populate and Clear Month move behind rehearsal mode

DECISIONS §45. Admin only; staff untouched at 27. No rules change, no Firebase console step.

WHY

  Owner, 16 Aug: "Auto-populate is funky. My original thought was that it would work as a
  simulator so I could rapidly assign shifts to test things. For this reason, I think it
  should move to a new testing section of admin, like the vacation site. Auto-pop month and
  year should move there. Let's stash clear month there as for now. Auto-pop for now should
  only give 1 assignment."

  It is not tidying. §44 records the target architecture: rules → requests → admin approval
  → an ENGINE builds the schedule. Auto-populate is a crude ancestor of that engine —
  greedy, single-pass, fairness-only — and the danger is growing it into the real thing by
  accretion. Moving it out of the daily-work pages puts a prototype where it belongs and
  stops a stray click on the Schedule Grid wiping a month.

WHAT MOVED

  Auto-Populate Month · Auto-Populate Year · Clear Month — all three off the Schedule Grid
  toolbar and onto a new Testing → 🎲 Simulator page. The Schedule Grid keeps a plain link
  saying where they went.

  The call-fairness baseline STAYS on the Schedule Grid: it is real fairness data feeding
  the Reports fairness view, not a testing tool. Its blurb no longer describes itself as
  belonging to auto-populate.

REHEARSAL MODE

  Copied from the Vacation Auction rather than invented — it already has a Testing nav
  heading, a Simulator, and one switch that arms every destructive testing tool, with a
  persistent red banner and blunt wording. The sites are deliberately convergent (§34).

  · Starts OFF. In memory, so it disarms on reload rather than remembering an unlock —
    the same reasoning as the Users-page lock in build 50.
  · Arming goes through a confirmation that says what it unlocks and that CLEAR MONTH HAS
    NO UNDO. Arming and disarming are both audited.
  · The refusal is at the top of all three handlers, not on the buttons. Calling
    autoPopulate() straight from the console while disarmed writes nothing and says so —
    a lock that only dims buttons is not a lock.
  · The banner and the page both state: this is NOT a sandbox. Everything writes the real
    schedule and staff see it immediately.

BEHAVIOUR CHANGE — one shift per person per day

  The simulator now gives each person AT MOST ONE shift on a day, total. Up to build 52 it
  gave one day shift AND one call. Owner's choice, asked explicitly: a simulator that can
  never double anyone up is easier to read while testing.

  Note this is the simulator's rule, not the schedule's. A person can still hold two day
  shifts — that is §8 and it shipped in 52; it is now only ever a deliberate human act.
