The smallest slice of stage 4 that rules need
Two things on a person, no more. Role is single-choice — §10 calls MD vs CRNA primary. Groups are named sets of people, and a person may be in any number — pediatric, obstetric, admin, call/non-call, per diem, locums, “and probably more.” Admin-created, not a fixed list in code, not a tree.

It is the same control you already have twice. Tags are named sets of shifts (shipped in 51); request targets are named sets of shifts; groups are named sets of people. One tick-list, three jobs.
Two things here need your ruling, not my guess (§22).
1. The current dropdown offers Both (MD & CRNA). Is that a third role, or a person who is in two groups? Defect 21 already flags that the hardcoded three-option list collides with the groups model.
2. “Counts toward fairness” lives on the group per §26 — “set on the group, not hard-coded.” Build 51 shipped that switch per person as an interim (§35), which already records that it moves onto the group when stage 4 lands, with the per-person value becoming an override rather than a second source of truth.
